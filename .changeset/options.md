A program declares a list-of-names option with `names` (comma-separated or repeated, completing the names not yet typed, strict if asked), resolves the names against its items with `selectNamed` or `selectOne`, and takes a duration-or-ISO time with `timestamp`. `/testing` adds `hermetic`, a config loader that sees only the variables a test gives it, `refused` and `writeUnder`.
